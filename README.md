Problem Statement

Traditional college canteens often face long queues, delayed food orders, difficulty in managing peak-hour demand, and food wastage due to inaccurate estimation of daily requirements. Students spend valuable time waiting to place and collect their orders, while canteen staff struggle to predict which food items will be in demand.

The proposed AI-Powered Smart Canteen Management and Food Demand Prediction System aims to provide a digital platform where students can view menus, pre-order food, and track their orders. The system also analyzes previous sales and ordering patterns to predict future food demand, helping canteen staff prepare the right quantity of food, reduce wastage, and improve overall service efficiency.
